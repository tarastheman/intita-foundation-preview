Developing the potential of people who shape the future of Ukraine

We believe that investing in education, science and human potential lays the foundation for Ukraine's long-term development. INTITA Foundation brings together resources, knowledge and partnerships to support educational opportunities, scientific research and initiatives capable of creating real change.

4 reasons to become a partner

We build cooperation on shared values, transparent communication and clear goals. The Foundation's partners can join initiatives aimed at developing Ukraine's intellectual potential and, by agreement, present their participation in the Foundation's public communications.

  • Public recognition of the partner's contribution
  • Participation in industry events and professional networking
  • Information on how support is used and what initiatives achieve
  • The option to choose a direction that matches the company's values

Privacy policy

INTITA Foundation

What data we collect

First name, last name, email, phone number, company or organisation name, position and request text — only what you entered in the form.

Why

To respond to your application and discuss support or cooperation with the Foundation. We do not use this data for anything else.

Who has access

The Foundation's team. We do not share data with third parties, except where required by law.

How long we keep it

While we are in contact about your application, and then no longer than needed for the Foundation's reporting.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data. Write to intitafoundation@gmail.com.
